In the hold · 1919-1923

What did the Black Star Line carry?

  1. ASPIRATION

    96,285 stockholders. The largest mass movement of African-descended people in the 20th century. Every voyage's hold carried people's idea of themselves first, then onions or whisky or lumber after.

  2. FRAUD

    A doctored photograph of someone else's ship. A 32-year-old hull purchased at six times appraisal. A federal indictment, a conviction, five years at Atlanta. The crime was the cargo.

  3. WHISKY

    800,000 bottles to Havana on the Yarmouth's maiden voyage, ahead of US Prohibition. Cuba had no Prohibition. New York had a tax loophole. Aspiration paid for the boiler with a tax loophole.

  4. FIRE

    A boiler that exploded three days out of Norfolk. A ship abandoned in Santiago de Cuba. Winter ice in 1922 that took the Shadyside down quietly at her mooring. The end was written into the beginning.

  5. A NAME

    Liberty Hall, Dublin 1916. Liberty Hall, Harlem 1919. Liberty Hall, Port Limón 1922. Liberty Hall, Puerto Viejo 2026. The room kept moving.

Voices · primary sources
HM
Hugh Mulzac
Chief officer, then captain · SS Yarmouth
b. 1886, St. Vincent · d. 1971

Sailed every Caribbean leg under Cockburn, then took the wheel. Wrote A Star to Steer By (1963), the only firsthand book-length account from the bridge. Later: first Black captain in the WWII US Merchant Marine.

Oral history · pending Black Star Archive
JC
Joshua Cockburn
Captain · SS Yarmouth maiden voyage, Nov 1919
Bahamian · first BSL master mariner

Commissioned the ship and ran the Havana whisky cargo. Eventually sidelined in financial disputes and fired. The complicated first hand on the wheel.

Source: Hill, UNIA Papers Vol. II
HD
Henrietta Vinton Davis
"Lady Commander" · highest-ranking woman in the UNIA

Spoke at Liberty Hall openings across the diaspora. Specific BSL voyages she sailed: pending cross-reference of Negro World arrival columns + Hill critical edition.
