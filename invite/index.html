Bring Legacy Lectures
into your room.

Legacy Lectures: Marcus Garvey is a free, six-week virtual series on Pan-Africanism, the UNIA, and the Black Star Line, taught from primary sources and hosted by Dr. Jewell Humphrey and Mike Spade. We're inviting educators, organizers, and institutions to either help shape a session or simply put it in front of the people you already reach.

Two ways in
Track 01

Collaborate

Co-present a session, bring a discussion group, contribute a primary source or a classroom activity, or help us think through a specific topic. We'll build the credit and the format around what you actually want to do.

Track 02

Share

Forward the series to your students, members, or network. No cost, no obligation. Just a real six-week course taught from the archive, ready to hand to people who'd want it.

For teachers

Assign a session as coursework, or use the primary sources directly: the Declaration of Rights, the Black Star Line brochure, the FBI file, as discussion material of your own.

For organizers

A ready-made study series for your members: Pan-Africanism as a living tradition, not a museum piece. Good for reading groups, chapter meetings, political education.

For institutions

A public-scholarship offering you can co-host, cross-post, or point your audience to: the same archive infrastructure behind the Garvey Living Archive (blackstararchive.com).

Not just a lecture series

Every session draws from the Marcus Garvey Living Archive: 37 primary sources, 618 passages, searchable in his own words. Try it yourself: garvey.radicalimagination.xyz/sam

This was never a US-only story

The UNIA's largest chapter network outside the United States was in Cuba, more than any other country. We're building a dedicated space for Caribbean and diaspora participants to connect directly with each other, not just watch. Details land soon, ask us about it now.
