## Correction (2026-10-10, caught by Mike)
The first version of this kit shipped a DIFFERENT certificate (1600×1361; from experiments/garvey-tour-teaser/assets/img/stock-cert.jpg, same file as experiments/garvey-fleet/assets/stock-certificate.jpg) under the Warner caption and Rblack131 credit. That certificate reads: "Mr. William Julius Hill … One share," dated 12 December 1921, capital stock $10,000,000, signed by an illegible Secretary-Treasurer and Marcus Garvey. Its source is UNRECORDED. It was removed from the voyage. Trace its provenance before using it anywhere with a credit (teaser and garvey-fleet both use it).
